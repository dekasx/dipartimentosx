Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This notice explains how the personal data of anyone visiting this site is handled, under art. 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”). It is written from how the site actually works: below you will only find what really happens when you open a page.

1. Data controller

Dipartimento SX, a collective of independent professionals based in Rome (Italy). For anything concerning personal data, including the rights described in section 8, write to: info@dipartimentosx.com. The controller’s full identification details are provided on request at the same address.

2. No cookies, no tracking

This site uses no cookies of any kind: not technical ones, not statistical ones, not profiling ones. It stores nothing in the browser (local storage, session storage, local databases). That is why no consent banner appears: there is nothing to consent to.

In particular, the site does not use:

  • analytics or traffic measurement tools;
  • tracking pixels, advertising networks, profiling;
  • contact forms, sign-up, private areas or newsletters;
  • fonts or libraries loaded from external servers: they are all hosted here;
  • embedded social network content. The links to Instagram and to client websites are ordinary links: until you open them, no request is made.

No special categories of data are processed (art. 9 GDPR).

3. Browsing data

Like any website, the servers that publish it automatically record the data whose transmission is implicit in the Internet protocols: IP address, date and time of the request, address of the requested page, outcome of the request, browser type and operating system. These serve to deliver the pages, keep the site secure and diagnose faults; the legal basis is the controller’s legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR).

These logs are generated and kept by the hosting provider according to its own technical retention times. We do not consult them to identify visitors, we do not export them and we do not cross-reference them with anything else.

4. Video

The videos on this site are not hosted here: they are on Cloudflare R2. When you open the home page the opening film starts on its own, so your browser connects to Cloudflare as soon as you arrive; the same happens in the catalogue when a video is played. At that moment Cloudflare receives, as any content delivery provider would, your IP address and the technical data of the request, and processes them to deliver the file and to keep its own network secure. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in making the content available (art. 6.1.f GDPR).

5. Email

If you write to info@dipartimentosx.com we process your address and the content of the message in order to reply and to handle what follows: the legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures or of the contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR), or the legitimate interest in replying (art. 6.1.f). The mailbox is provided by Hostinger and messages are forwarded to Google (Gmail). Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the request and the relationship that follows, and for subsequent legal obligations.

6. Providers

The data described above is processed, on our behalf or within their own remit, by the technical providers that make the site possible:

  • Netlify — publishing the site and server logs;
  • Cloudflare — video delivery;
  • Hostinger — the mailbox;
  • Google — the inbox messages are forwarded to.

We do not sell, transfer or disclose data to third parties for commercial purposes, and we do not use it to profile anyone.

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Some of these providers are based in the United States or may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case the transfer takes place on the basis of the safeguards set out in Chapter V of the GDPR: the European Commission’s adequacy decision on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, or the standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission.

8. Your rights

At any time you can ask for access to your data, its rectification or erasure, the restriction of processing, portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest (arts. 15-22 GDPR). Just write to info@dipartimentosx.com. If you believe the processing breaches the GDPR you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

9. Minors

The site is not aimed at minors and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

This notice may change if the site changes. Any changes are published on this page, with the last-updated date shown above.
